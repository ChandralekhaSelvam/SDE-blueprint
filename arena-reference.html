Model benchmarking, run live — not a static leaderboard

This is the model-benchmark step of the DoD: 15 forecasting methods scored under identical data, identical hardware and identical rolling-origin evaluation criteria (below). Nothing here is precomputed into a table — you watch the same six backtest rounds the methods actually ran, the battle log records every result the moment it becomes statistically significant, and you can re-project the same population onto whichever question matters: who wins head-to-head, who's biased which direction, who earns their compute, or who actually moves plan margin.
